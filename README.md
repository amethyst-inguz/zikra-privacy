# Privacy Policy for Zikra

_Last updated: 30-09-2026_

Zikra ("the app") is developed by Amethyst Inguz. This policy explains
what data the app handles.

## Data the app does not collect

Zikra does not have user accounts, does not use analytics or crash-reporting
SDKs, does not show ads, and does not share, sell, or transmit any personal
data to us or to any third party. There is no server that stores your
information — the app has no backend at all for your data.

## Data stored on your device only

The following stays entirely on your device, in local storage, and is never
transmitted anywhere:
- Your chosen language, theme, and display preferences
- Your day-window schedule (custom times, or prayer-time mode)
- Your azkar completion history, streaks, and reminder settings

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this. Nothing is backed up to any server
operated by us.

## Location (optional)

If you enable "Follow prayer times" and choose "Use my location," the app
reads your device's approximate (coarse) location once, to compute prayer
times for your area. This calculation happens entirely on-device using an
offline astronomical library — your location is never sent to us or to any
third party, and is not stored beyond the single reading used to compute the
prayer schedule you can already see in the app. You can instead pick a city
manually and skip location access entirely.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your
device via Android/iOS system APIs. This does not involve any push
notification service or external server — no notification content or
schedule ever leaves your device.

## Network access

The only network request Zikra makes is an optional check against a static
update-info file, to tell you if a newer version is available. This request
does not include any personal or device-identifying information beyond what
any HTTP request inherently carries (IP address, at the network level, not
logged or stored by us). All azkar content is bundled with the app and
requires no network access to use.
